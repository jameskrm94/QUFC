Face-Off Scoring Calibration

This page runs the calibration and tuning system already sitting inside faceoff-calibration.js against the real Hall of Faces archive photos, so you can see how far off the current Ugly Score thresholds are — and find better ones — before touching a single number in the live code.

Step 1 — Score the archive against today's config

Runs the current scoringConfig against all six Hall of Faces normal/ugly pairs and compares the result to the score you originally targeted for each one.

Step 2 — Record negative controls

The parameter sweep in Step 3 needs to know what an ordinary face looks like too — otherwise a config that happens to fit the six archive photos could also start scoring a yawn or a laugh as "ugly." Record three quick reference faces with your camera. Nothing here is uploaded or saved anywhere — it stays in this browser tab and disappears when you close it.

  • Relaxed / Neutral Face (also becomes the baseline for the other two)
  • Normal Smile
  • Simple Expression

Step 3 — Run the parameter sweep

Tests 375 combinations of the tunable scoring thresholds against the archive photos and the negative controls above, then ranks them by how closely they match your targets without also flagging an ordinary face as ugly.

Needs Step 1 and all three Step 2 captures first.
