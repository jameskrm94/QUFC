How to participate in important science

Three highly unnecessary steps.

Make any ugly face

No assigned pose. No difficulty. Your only job is to make your face behave irresponsibly.

Capture the evidence

Open the camera, commit fully to the mistake, and snap a photo you may someday deny existed.

Challenge somebody

Send your Face-Off card to a friend or family member and dare them to make an uglier one.

Your face, your terrible decisions

No prompts. That's the point.

QUFC is about creativity, comedy, and discovering what your own face is capable of. A Face-Off does not tell you what expression to make. The freedom to invent something uniquely unfortunate is the competition.

1. Make it your own.

Squint your eyes, have 5 chins, or look like somehow caught both rabies and Alzeihmers. Your only limit is your mind.

2. Overdo it.

Nothing is too extreme. Push the boundaraies and limits to their maximum. If your face hurts or feels sore, your doing it right.

3. Keep it friendly.

QUFC is about funny/ridiculous expressions, not judging anybody's normal day-to-day appearance. Challenge your family, friends, coworkers, etc..... and have fun!

A note about “ugly”

Ridiculous expressions, not real appearances.

QUFC is about deliberately making funny, strange, exaggerated faces. It is not about judging anyone's normal appearance. The goal is creativity, comedy, and making your own face behave irresponsibly for a few seconds.
