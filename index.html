How it began

Sibling torment became a tradition.

For years, the Martin siblings made horrifying faces at one another for no particularly good reason. One Christmas Eve.... the eldest and wisest, Nicole, turned the chaos into an actual contest: submit one face, vote for the ugliest, and immortalize the winner.

Then 2020 arrived. Quarantine created the perfect conditions for the competition to return with a website and an unnecessarily official name: The Quarantine Ugly Face Contest.

A note about “ugly”

Ridiculous faces, not real appearances.

Everyone here is voluntarily trying to make the most outrageous face possible. QUFC is about creativity, comedy, and family competition—not judging anyone's normal day-to-day appearance.
